Privacy policy for Noor Quest

App name: Noor Quest
Package name: com.lefgiak.noorquest
Developer: Lef Giak
Last updated: 22 November 2026

A short summary

Noor Quest is a children’s faith quiz game. It is designed to work fully offline. It does not create accounts, show advertisements, or collect personal data. Progress and settings stay on the device. The app is designed not to make network calls while it is being played.

Who this policy is for

This policy is for parents and guardians of children who use Noor Quest, and for anyone who installs the app. The app is made for children aged 6 to 10.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, share or sell personal information. In particular, it does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or photos
  • account or login details (there is no account)
  • location
  • contacts, calendars or files
  • payment information (there are no purchases)
  • advertising identifiers or other persistent device identifiers
  • analytics, crash reports or usage logs sent off the device

The app does not include advertisements, and it does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information that stays on the device

The app stores, on the device only:

  • quiz progress, stars, lanterns and garden state, for each faith the child has played
  • settings chosen in the parent area, such as sound, read-aloud, larger text and whether levels are unlocked

This information is used only to keep the child’s place in the game and to remember those settings. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party. A parent can clear progress from the parent area, behind the parent gate. Uninstalling the app also removes this information from the device.

Read-aloud

If read-aloud is switched on, the app uses the device’s own text-to-speech to speak questions and Learn more passages. The app does not send that text to the developer. Some devices can be set, by the parent, to use a cloud speech service provided by the device maker. That choice belongs to the device, not to Noor Quest, and is covered by the device maker’s own policy.

Learn more passages

Some answers include a short sourced passage. Those texts are bundled inside the app. They come from public-domain or CC0 translations. Credits are listed in the parent area. The app does not fetch new passages from the internet while it is being played.

Children

Noor Quest is directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone else, through this app. A parent gate protects grown-up settings so that children are not taken to adult-only options by accident.

Permissions and internet use

The app is designed to work offline and not to make network calls at runtime. If a future version needs the internet, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Play Store Data safety form will be updated to match.

Sharing

We do not share user information with third parties, because the app does not collect user information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. If a change affects how information is handled, the new policy will be posted before the changed app version is made available.
